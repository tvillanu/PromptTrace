Prompt Trace

Version 1.0.0

Effortlessly track, audit, and log your AI prompts and responses across platforms, then export your history to CSV anytime with full control over your data.

Click Export below to download a CSV file viewable in Excel or any spreadsheet app.

Click Clear below to clear the cache of all logged prompts and responses.

To contribute to this Github project, visit PromptTrace

Note: PromptTrace does not store any data on external servers. All logged prompts and responses are stored locally in your browser's cache.
